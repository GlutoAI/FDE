# Collections and approval policy

SYNTHETIC DATA — all entities and events are fictional.

Document ID: collections-policy-tenant_aurora
Tenant ID: tenant_aurora
Effective date: 2026-07-01

Policy version 1, approved by the fictional business owner.

1. Read the current ledger before drafting or sending a reminder.
2. Any open dispute places the whole invoice on a reminder hold until a human resolves it, even if only part of the balance is disputed.
3. Every external message requires an authorized human's approval of its exact recipient and content. Editing a draft invalidates its previous approval.
4. Recheck balance, dispute status, data freshness, and permissions immediately before sending. Changed facts require a new draft and approval.
5. Do not add unapproved late fees, invent payment commitments, or expose another business's records.
6. Use respectful language. Escalate missing evidence to a human.
7. Data older than 60 minutes cannot authorize a new external action.
8. Do not automatically repeat an action with an uncertain delivery result; reconcile with the provider or request operator review.
